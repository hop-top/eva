# Marketing Brief — Eva

**One-Liner**: Eva is the enforcement layer for AI agent behavior, ensuring every interaction meets your standards.

---

## 💎 The Four Pillars of Value

1.  **Trust through Transparency**: Move beyond "vibes-based" evaluations. Eva provides quantitative, repeatable evidence that your agents are performing as expected.
2.  **Developer Experience First**: Define behavioral expectations in human-readable YAML. Eva integrates seamlessly into your local development loop and your CI/CD pipeline.
3.  **Proactive Enforcement**: Eva doesn't just watch; it enforces. Catch malformed requests and poor-quality responses before they ever reach your customers.
4.  **Ecosystem Agnostic**: Built on industry-standard protocols (AGNTCY, OASF, ACP), Eva works with any agent, any model, and any platform.

---

## 🎯 Positioning

Eva is for **engineering teams** that are moving AI agents from experiments into production. It addresses the gap between raw model capabilities and the strict reliability requirements of real-world software.

---
